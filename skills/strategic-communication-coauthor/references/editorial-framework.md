# Editorial framework

Use this reference when deciding whether and how a contribution fits 《AI战略传播力》.

## The book's central question

In an AI-mediated communication environment, how can a nation, city, or enterprise move beyond one-off exposure and build repeatable strategic communication power — being perceived, understood, trusted, and chosen, with attention converted into lasting competitiveness?

## Core framework: five dimensions in one

| Dimension | Central question | Editorial test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 传播主体 | 谁在传播——人机共生的主体格局如何成立？ | Does the proposal clarify human–AI subject relations without mythologizing or dismissing AI? |
| 传播机制 | 如何传播——机制链条是否成立？ | Does it strengthen data as the base, the human–AI–human path, or trust as the core? |
| 传播目标 | 为何传播——四级跃迁是否推进？ | Does it advance being perceived → understood → trusted → chosen? |
| 传播体系 | 如何运转——闭环是否可持续？ | Does it improve the insight–generation–distribution–interaction loop or the new assessment standards? |
| 传播分层 | 在哪传播——三层是否协同？ | Does it clarify the division and synergy among the national, city, and enterprise layers? |

The wider architecture is:

```text
认知主权
  ↓
AI战略传播力：五维一体 × 四级跃迁
  ↓
国家层（合法性）· 城市层（吸引力）· 企业层（可信性）
  ↓
从注意力到竞争力的可持续转化
```

## Fit tests

A useful proposal should clarify a core concept, add verifiable evidence or a boundary, connect one of the five dimensions to a real decision, improve an implementation tool, distinguish short-lived exposure from repeatable capability, or show where the framework needs qualification.

A proposal is not ready when it mainly adds publicity language, repeats the manuscript, invents precise metrics, treats model output or platform popularity as evidence, or promotes a nation, city, enterprise, or vendor without a transferable lesson.

## Evidence hierarchy

Prefer current primary material: laws and standards, official statistics, original research, audited disclosures, first-party technical documentation, and directly observable public records. Secondary analysis may provide context. Social posts, testimonials, search snippets, AI answers, publicity material, and vendor marketing are leads rather than sufficient proof for material claims.

For each important source, capture who published it, what it directly supports, when it was published or retrieved, its geographic and temporal scope, reuse constraints, and limitations.

## Editorial status language

- `proposed`: submitted to the public proposal library, not yet substantively reviewed;
- `under-review`: maintainers are evaluating the proposal;
- `selected`: maintainers intend to incorporate all or part of it, subject to editing;
- `incorporated`: a separate editorial Pull Request has merged material into `book/`.

Do not describe a proposal as author-approved, selected, incorporated, or published unless the repository records that state.
